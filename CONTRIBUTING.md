## Contributing to Fez / Transbot
Thank you for contributing to Fez / Transbot! Your efforts are greatly appreciated. Below are some guidelines for contributing.

## Rules for Contributions
1. Do not use generative AI for any part of your contributions. We reserve the right to deny any pull requests if we suspect AI has been used.
2. Do not open PRs for image, meme, or joke commands. They will not be accepted, and they will take valuable time away from the project's maintainers.  A maintainer will communicate with you in advance if a situation exists that allows for an exception to this policy.
